WHY 100 Hz

Gu et al. 2025

Motion sickness is driven by vestibular dysfunction. The otolith organs of the inner ear — the utricle and saccule — carry tiny calcium-carbonate crystals called otoconia that sense linear acceleration. A 100 Hz pure tone is mechanically transduced by those crystals: it activates the vestibular end organs rather than the cochlea alone. A single 1-minute exposure before motion was enough to reduce postural imbalance, autonomic dysregulation and subjective symptoms in mice and in humans.

sound pressure level LZ = 20 · log10( p / p₀ ) p₀ = 20 µPa
A-weighting at 100 Hz LA = LZ − 19.1 dB ⇒ 80–85 dB(Z) = 60.9–65.9 dB(A)

Protocol bilateral (both ears), 1 minute, before the ride. In mice the effect of a 5-minute exposure persisted for ≥ 120 min. Unilateral exposure and 250 Hz did not work.

Gu Y, Ohgami N, He T, Kagawa T, Kurniasari F, Tong K, Li X, Tazaki A, Takeda K, Mouri M, Kato M. Just 1-min exposure to a pure tone at 100 Hz with daily exposable sound pressure levels may improve motion sickness. Environ Health Prev Med. 2025;30. doi:10.1265/ehpm.24-00247

使用说明

  1. 把手机放在身边（口袋里也可），让 100 Hz 纯音持续播放。
  2. 音量请用手机侧边音量键调整（App 内不设音量条），由小到大调到清楚可闻、不刺耳；对照论文，大约是耳旁 80~85 dB(Z)，也就是 60.9~65.9 dB(A)。
  3. 论文方案是出发前 1 分钟、双耳同时听；默认时长已设为 60 秒，也可切 20/40 秒或循环。
  4. 播放中屏幕会保持常亮；锁屏或切到别的 App 音频会继续（可下拉控制中心暂停）。
  5. 若听不到声音，请检查 iPhone 左侧的静音拨片/静音开关是否关闭。
