WHY 100 Hz

Gu et al. 2025

Motion sickness follows vestibular dysfunction. A pure tone at 100 Hz is transduced by the otoconia — the calcium-carbonate crystals of the utricle and saccule — so it activates the otolith organs, not just the cochlea.

level LZ = 20 · log10( p / p₀ ) p₀ = 20 µPa
A-weighting LA = LZ − 19.1 dB ⇒ 80–85 dB(Z) = 60.9–65.9 dB(A)
dose texposure = 1 min before motion, bilaterally

Protocolbilateral, 1 minute, before motion. In mice a 5-minute exposure held for ≥ 120 min; unilateral exposure and 250 Hz did not work.

Gu Y, Ohgami N, He T, Kagawa T, Kurniasari F, Tong K, Li X, Tazaki A, Takeda K, Mouri M, Kato M. Just 1-min exposure to a pure tone at 100 Hz with daily exposable sound pressure levels may improve motion sickness. Environ Health Prev Med. 2025;30. doi:10.1265/ehpm.24-00247

Long exposure at high volume can damage hearing — keep both moderate. Stop immediately if you notice ringing, worsening dizziness or any discomfort. This app is not medical advice.

Instructions

  1. Keep the phone near you — a pocket is fine — and let the 100 Hz tone play.
  2. Use the hardware volume buttons (there is no in-app volume). Aim for clearly audible but never uncomfortable: the study used 80–85 dB(Z) ≈ 60.9–65.9 dB(A) at the ear.
  3. The study protocol is 1 minute, both ears, before the ride. The default session is 60 s; 20 s, 40 s and loop are also available.
  4. The screen stays awake while playing. Audio keeps playing on the lock screen or in another app — pause it from Control Center.
  5. No sound? Check the ring/silent switch on the left side of the iPhone.
